Frequently Asked Questions

What areas in Ujjain do you cover?

We cover major prime localities including Nanakheda, Jawahar Nagar, Malipura, Freeganj, Mahananda Nagar, Indore-Ujjain Highway, Dewas Road, and Agar Road.

Do you charge any brokerage fees?

Yes, we charge a standard and completely transparent brokerage fee based on the transaction value. All our fees are discussed upfront before any agreement is signed.

How can I list my property with you?

You can easily list your property by clicking on the 'Post Property Free' button in our menu, or by filling out the contact form with your property details. Our agent will verify and list it for you.

Do you help with legal documentation and registry?

Yes! We provide full end-to-end support including legal verification, documentation preparation, and complete assistance during the property registry process.

Can NRIs buy property through your firm?

Absolutely. We offer specialized international property services for NRIs looking to invest in Ujjain, ensuring a smooth remote transaction process.

What types of properties do you deal in?

We deal in a wide variety of properties including Residential (Flats, Villas, Plots), Commercial (Shops, Offices), and Agricultural lands across all our 19 service types.
