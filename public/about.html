Our Story

Shri Manibhadra Real Estate Brokers was founded with a clear vision: to bring transparency, trust, and professionalism to Ujjain's rapidly booming real estate market. We recognized the need for a reliable advisory service that puts the client's interests first.

Located centrally at Shop 14, Shyama Prasad Mukherjee Complex, Nanakheda — the commercial heart of Ujjain — we are perfectly positioned to serve our clients. Our strategic location allows us to stay connected with the city's pulse and fastest-growing corridors.

Our team comprises experienced property consultants with deep, hyper-local knowledge of every locality including Nanakheda, Jawahar Nagar, Malipura, Freeganj, Mahananda Nagar, and the high-potential Indore-Ujjain Highway.

We stand by our commitment to 100% legal verification, transparent pricing, and a client-first service approach that ensures peace of mind in every transaction.
